During our city tour, as we all sat in a circle on the warm grass of a bright May day, we shared our initial concepts for the Audiowalk topic. Hearing that several classmates had decided to dive into the historical aspects of Braunschweig made me reflect on alternative narratives. I realised that other, darker layers coexist in the shadows of the city's vibrant streets and tourist landmarks. I became intrigued by locations that look perfectly welcoming and peaceful during the day, yet transform into their complete opposite once night falls.

To identify such a spot, I recalled personal experiences where I felt unsettled or noticed I was being followed. For these situations, no external research is required; however, before finalising the location, I still attempted to cross-reference town archives to see if any criminal activity had been officially documented there. 

This led me to focus on a path called Kirschbaumallee. When I investigated its local background across multiple search engines like Ecosia and DuckDuckGo and platforms like Reddit, the digital archives revealed that it has no recorded history of crime and is not officially designated as an "Angstraum" (space of fear). Instead, it is a relatively new, modern urban development that is also known locally as the Eulenspiegeltwete. 

It remains a very young place in comparison to historic avenues like Jasperallee or Kastanienallee, which carry deep historical roots. This specific passage is deeply connected to local terminology, where Eulenspiegel refers to Till Eulenspiegel, the famous trickster figure from German folklore known for his satirical pranks, and a twete is a traditional Northern German dialect word for a very narrow alleyway or a small passage between buildings.

Understanding that this location's official history is practically "clean," I decided to document the area on camera during the late evening. Before writing the final script, I had to physically walk through the passage to actively recall the emotions and discomfort I felt when those disturbing situations originally occurred. This immersion was exactly what inspired me to draft the narrative text that will serve as the voiceover for my upcoming website interface.
